Before you arrive

Important reminders.

Is there a registration fee?

No, participation is completely free of cost.

What is the dress code?

Strict formal dress is required. Bring your school ID card; students without an ID card will not be allowed to enter campus.

What about transport and food?

Transport is not provided. Please arrange your own travel and bring a tiffin box, as lunch is not provided.

Can parents attend?

Parents are welcome to join as delegates. They will have separate Baraza discussions and activities throughout the day.

When is the delegate orientation?

Delegate orientation will be held in the auditorium next week. Further details will be shared later.
