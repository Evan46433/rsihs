The global community

What is Round Square?

Round Square is an international community of more than 250 schools across 50 countries and six continents. Together, these schools help students develop self-awareness, leadership, and empathy, with the confidence to solve real-world problems.

Its philosophy is inspired by German educator Kurt Hahn, who believed that education should reveal the potential already within every person.

“There is more in us than we know. If we could be made to see it, perhaps for the rest of our lives we would be unwilling to settle for less.” — Kurt Hahn

Our host

Organized by The Indian High School, Dubai, at the Oud Metha campus.

The big question

Are we happy only because we feel good?

This conference explores how negative emotions give contrast to our experiences and how we can curate a balanced mental ecosystem.

Our inspiration

The in-person event builds on the success of “Happiness Hacks: What Makes You Smile,” an online conference held on August 19, 2026, with more than 100 students worldwide across 10 Barazas.

This time, online screens become real physical connection, spontaneity, and shared immersion.
